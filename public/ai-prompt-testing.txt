6. Team composition:
  The team roster is specified in the team_rosters table. Each team must have exactly 1 player for each position: PG, SG, SF, PF, C, G.
  Ideally each team should have 2 players that are eligible for each position. Some players are eligible for multiple positions.
  For example, James Harden is eligible for PG, SG, and G. Multiple position eligibity is better than single position eligibity since it'll allow the player to be slotted into multiple positions on the team.
  When recommending players, consider the team composition and the best players for each position.
